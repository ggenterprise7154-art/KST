QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

A few things you may want to know.

Every project starts with a conversation. Here are answers to a few questions people commonly ask before taking the next step.

Visit our FAQs
Who can work with Kingdom Spirit Technology?

We welcome enquiries from individuals, entrepreneurs, startups, small businesses and established organisations. We will discuss your needs and confirm whether the requested work is a suitable fit.

Can I request a service that is not listed?

Yes. Tell us about the problem you are trying to solve. We can review the request and discuss whether it fits our capabilities or requires another approach.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, features, integrations and requirements of each project. Share a brief description and we can discuss the appropriate next step.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Many digital projects can be planned and delivered remotely. Communication, milestones and handover arrangements will be agreed before work begins.

What happens after I make an enquiry?

We review the information you provide, discuss the project with you and outline possible next steps, including scope and a quotation where appropriate.
