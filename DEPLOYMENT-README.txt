KINGDOM SPIRIT TECHNOLOGY WEBSITE — COMPLETED PAGE CONTENT

The website homepage is index.html at the top level. Shared style.css, script.js and images/ are beside it. Deploy the contents of this folder to Netlify (the folder that directly contains index.html).

All HTML pages have been populated with responsive page content. Seven core service departments have individual pages with sub-services and indicative starting prices in NGN. No listed price is ₦150,000 or above; the highest listed starting price is ₦149,000. Prices are indicative and final quotations depend on scope.

IMPORTANT FUNCTIONALITY NOTES
- The enquiry forms are marked for Netlify Forms, but form handling must be enabled and tested in your Netlify site settings.
- Client login, registration, password recovery, client dashboard and admin pages are interface foundations only. Connect a secure authentication/database backend before using them with real accounts or confidential information.
- Payment page is informational only; no payment is collected. Integrate and test a payment provider before enabling checkout.
- Legal pages contain starter wording and should be reviewed against your actual business practices and applicable law before publication.

To deploy: extract this ZIP, open the extracted folder, confirm index.html is directly inside it, then upload/deploy that folder to Netlify.
